🌟 About Samartha

Empowering Businesses Through Intelligent Automation

We're on a mission to make powerful automation accessible to every business, regardless of size or technical expertise.

What We Stand For

Mission & Vision

The purpose and direction that drives everything we build.

Our Mission

To democratize business automation by providing intuitive, AI-powered tools that help teams work smarter, not harder. We believe every business deserves access to enterprise-grade automation without the enterprise-level complexity.

Our Vision

A world where businesses spend zero time on repetitive tasks and 100% time on innovation and growth. We're building the future where AI handles the mundane, freeing humans to focus on creativity and strategic thinking.

Our Journey

How Samartha Started

From a small startup to powering automation for thousands of businesses worldwide

2020

The Beginning

Founded by a team of automation enthusiasts who saw businesses struggling with repetitive manual tasks. We started with a simple idea: make automation accessible to everyone.

2021

First 100 Customers

Reached our first milestone with 100 happy customers automating their workflows. Launched our visual workflow builder and integrated with 50+ apps.

2023

AI Integration

Introduced AI-powered automation features, enabling intelligent decision-making and predictive workflows. Expanded to 500+ integrations.

2024

Global Expansion

Crossed 5,000 businesses using Samartha across 50+ countries. Launched enterprise features and dedicated support teams.

2026

Today & Beyond

Continuing to innovate with cutting-edge AI, helping 10,000+ businesses automate 10M+ tasks monthly. The journey is just beginning.
